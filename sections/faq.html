Frequently Asked Questions

Clear guidance for horse owners, riders, and equestrian professionals.

Are the patches safe and legal for competition?

Phototherapy patches are non-invasive and contain no drugs. Check the requirements of the relevant governing body and consult your veterinary professional before use in a competition setting.

How does phototherapy work for horses?

Placement protocols are inspired by phototherapy and acupressure principles. Explore our education resources or speak with our team for product-specific guidance.

Where do I place the patches?

Placement depends on the product and protocol. Your purchase includes placement guidance, and our professional resources provide additional support.

Can riders use EquineVital products too?

Yes. We provide rider-focused product information and protocols for everyday performance, recovery, travel, sleep, and stress support.

How does the Professional Partner programme work?

Approved partners receive training, practical resources, and ongoing support to recommend products confidently within their existing business or professional service.
