Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EquineVital patches and partnerships.

Are the patches safe and legal for competition?

Yes. Our phototherapy patches are completely non-invasive and contain no drugs or chemicals. They are widely used in professional racing, show jumping, and dressage, complying with major equine competition standards.

How exactly does phototherapy work for horses?

The patches use specialized nanocrystals that reflect specific wavelengths of the horse's own body heat (infrared light) back into their body. This gently stimulates the skin to support natural energy flow, recovery, and comfort.

Where do I place the patches?

Placement depends on the specific protocol (e.g., whole-body recovery vs. localized comfort). When you purchase, you will receive a comprehensive placement guide and access to our video library showing exact acupressure points.

Can these be used on humans too?

Absolutely. We offer dedicated protocols and patches specifically designed for riders to help manage travel fatigue, focus, and recovery after long days in the saddle.

How does the Professional Partner program work?

As a partner, you receive wholesale pricing, dedicated training, and resources to offer our solutions directly to your clients. It is designed for vets, trainers, and stable owners looking to add a natural recovery solution to their business while creating an additional revenue stream.
