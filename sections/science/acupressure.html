Understanding Acupressure

Acupressure-inspired placement principles guide the strategic positioning of phototherapy patches on horses and riders.

Drawing from traditional Eastern medicine concepts, these protocols target specific points to support balance and natural wellness. The approach is non-invasive and avoids pharmaceutical interventions.

For equine athletes, this means targeted support that aligns with the body's own energy systems, helping maintain comfort and performance through natural methods.
