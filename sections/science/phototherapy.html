Understanding Phototherapy

Photobiomodulation (PBM), or low-level light therapy, utilizes specific wavelengths of light to modulate cellular activity.

Research indicates that light is absorbed by mitochondrial photoreceptors, such as cytochrome c oxidase, which helps promote cellular energy (ATP) production and supports endogenous repair processes without causing thermal damage.

When applied to equine care, this non-invasive approach provides a safe, drug-free pathway to support natural healing, reduce inflammation, and enhance overall vitality.
